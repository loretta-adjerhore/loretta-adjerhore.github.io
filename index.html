Featured Projects

🏥 GenexaHealth | Warfarin Precision Dosing

Healthcare Analytics Internship

Analyzed 50,000 patient records to identify adverse event patterns in warfarin dosing, revealing 10-fold dose variability invalidating standard protocols. Developed risk stratification framework identifying high-risk groups accounting for 50%+ of adverse events.

Key Objectives
  • Identify patterns in adverse warfarin events across patient populations
  • Evaluate dosing protocol effectiveness and dose variability
  • Develop risk stratification framework for high-risk patients
  • Quantify potential cost savings from improved monitoring
Key Insights
  • Discovered 10-fold dose variability (1mg-10mg) indicating critical protocol gaps
  • 48.6% of patients required doses significantly below standard 5mg starting dose
  • High-risk groups (CKD patients, elderly 76+, amiodarone users) accounted for 50%+ of adverse events
  • Lifestyle factors (smoking + alcohol) created 5.5x risk amplification
  • Identified $20-100M in preventable cost savings through precision dosing
Tools: Power BI, DAX, Healthcare Analytics Impact: $20-100M savings potential 25%+ adverse event reduction

👥 NextGen Corp | Employee Success Analytics

HR Analytics & SQL Case Study

Analyzed employee database (197 employees, 89 turnovers) to investigate turnover crisis. Identified Engineering department existential risk (150% turnover rate) and systematic pay-performance inversion affecting retention.

Key Insights
  • Engineering department showed 150% turnover rate (existential threat - only 6 engineers remain)
  • Systematic pay-performance inversion: Marketing (4.13 score, $77K) vs Sales (4.00 score, $84K)
  • Engineers underpaid $15-40K below market ($80K actual vs $95-120K market rate)
  • Identified 7 high-risk employees requiring immediate retention intervention
  • Zero correlation between compensation and performance driving top talent departure
Tools: SQL, PostgreSQL, HR Analytics Impact: $800K-$1.2M savings opportunity 3-4x ROI on adjustments
